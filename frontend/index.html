🎮 HOW TO PLAY SPATIAL QUEST

Welcome Investigator! Step into Sector 4, a 3D research facility powered by a custom-trained Computer Vision detector and an in-house Transformer Language Model.

1. Explore the Facility: Move your character using W, A, S, D or on-screen D-Pad. Drag mouse to look around. Explore Reception, Office, Library, Laboratory, and Storage Vault.
2. Ask Your AI Companion: Type commands or click the 🎙️ Microphone to speak (e.g. "What can you see?", "Where is the nearest table?", "Take me to the library.").
3. Inspect Objects & Collect Clues: Walk near tables, wall safes, and datapads. Press E or tell your companion "Inspect the datapad note" to find passwords.
4. Follow Evidence & Solve Mysteries: Use local RAG document queries (e.g. "Search documents for override code") to unlock security doors and resolve incidents.
5. Complete & Switch Mysteries: Click 🗺️ SELECT MYSTERY at any time to freely choose Mystery A, B, or C, or roll a 🎲 NEW MYSTERY with a fresh seed.

❓ MISSION GUIDE & INVESTIGATION CHEAT SHEET

Spatial Quest is an Embodied AI Companion Investigation Game. You don't have to search for keypads on 3D walls! You control the investigation by typing commands to your companion drones (Scout, Navigator, Archivist) or speaking via microphone.
🔢 WHERE & HOW TO ENTER CODES
When you find a security code (such as 7391 for Archive Door 3):
  • Type directly into the chat: Enter code 7391 or Unlock Door 3 with code 7391
  • Or click the quick button: 🔢 Enter Code 7391 below the chat!
  • Your companion will execute the security override and disengage Door 3's magnetic lock.
🔑 WHERE & HOW TO GET KEYS
In Mystery A, the Master Research Key is stored inside the facility cabinet:
  • Type in chat: Open the cabinet or Pick up the key
  • Or click 🗄️ Open Cabinet / Key in the quick prompts bar!
  • Or walk near the cabinet in 3D and press E on your keyboard.
  • The key will automatically be collected into your inventory.
🎯 CHECKING MISSION PROGRESSION
  • Mission Board: Shows real-time checklist: [✓] COMPLETED vs [○] PENDING.
  • Ask Drone: Type Have I completed my mission? or What should I do next?.
  • Completion Banner: When all objectives are done, a green 🎉 MISSION ACCOMPLISHED banner appears with a ▶ NEXT MYSTERY button to progress immediately!
