=== SPATIAL QUEST INVESTIGATION FACILITY CORPUS ===

[FACILITY ARCHITECTURE AND LAYOUT]
The Sector 4 Advanced Research Facility is divided into five interconnected spatial zones:
Location 1: Reception / Central Hub. Contains the primary reception table, visitor sofa, security check scanner, security desk, and the main double entry door.
Location 2: Research Office. Contains research desks, rolling office chairs, filing cabinets, computer terminals, and office bookshelves.
Location 3: Library / Archives. Contains historical case files on towering wooden bookshelves, electronic lockboxes, Datapad terminals, and Archive Door 3.
Location 4: Investigation Laboratory. Contains heavy workbenches, laboratory tables, sample cabinets, chemical apparatus, and safety wash stations.
Location 5: Storage Room & Vault. Contains heavy reinforced vault doors, secure wall safes, storage containers, supply crates, and security lockers.

[SPATIAL GEOMETRY AND COORDINATE RULES]
The companion drone utilizes an egocentric 3D coordinate frame where +X points right, +Y points up, and +Z points forward in depth.
When the drone perceives an object at coordinate [x, y, z]:
- If x < -0.4 meters, the object is located to the left of the drone center.
- If x > 0.4 meters, the object is located to the right of the drone center.
- If -0.4 <= x <= 0.4 meters, the object is directly ahead.
- The Euclidean distance between object A at [x1, y1, z1] and object B at [x2, y2, z2] is sqrt((x1-x2)^2 + (y1-y2)^2 + (z1-z2)^2).
- Spatial relationships include: left_of, right_of, near (distance < 2.5 meters), above (y1 > y2 + 0.5 meters), and on.

Incident Report 104-A states that a power surge tripped the magnetic locks on Archive Door 3, putting Sector 4 into lockdown.
Safety Protocol 22 specifies that hazardous chemical vapor requires donning Level B respirators and turning on ventilation scrubbers at the console.
The manual emergency override passcode for Door 3 is 7391. The physical emergency key is hidden in the steel cabinet beside the bookshelf.
Artifact Omega radiates non-ionizing electromagnetic interference within 0.5 meters. Titanium tongs must be used during handling.
